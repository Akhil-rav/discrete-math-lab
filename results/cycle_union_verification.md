# Even-cycle unions: checks

For every nonempty fixed union G of cycles C(2n_i), with r>=1,
all n_i>=2, and m=2 sum n_i, the maximum avoiding color count is m-2. Every
extremizer has two repeated pairs in one component, one inside each alternating
matching, and all other classes singleton. Its fixed-edge partition count is
sum_i binomial(n_i,2)^2.

The complete argument is [Theorem 4 in the reading copy](../notes/cycle_extremizers.md),
with a corresponding [LaTeX theorem](../papers/cycle_extremizers.tex).

SHA-256: `b77c5598b5527277c47ef72050a395a3e3241c80a7a285fdbfe0b7c434c59d8b`

The complete proof is in the linked note and paper. The experiment below
checks the construction and compares all extremizers on the specified hosts.

On all ten hosts the search enumerates every partition with m-2, m-1, or m
classes. Coverage counts agree with an independent Stirling recurrence.
The avoiding set equals the construction's complete set, with no duplicates;
all 441 extremizers across the ten fixed hosts are saved. The six hosts
through ten edges also receive full Bell-number enumeration with a different
iterator and an equal-color-pair predicate rather than a distinct-color-set
predicate. Their full extremal sets and maximum counts agree exactly.

There are **240,448 full-search candidates** and **9,365 band-search
candidates**. These are counts from separate passes with overlap, not a
claim that their sum counts distinct partitions. The twelve-edge hosts do
not receive full Bell-number enumeration.

Global matchings are generated by independent alternating choices, then
checked against every half-size edge subset that uses each vertex once.
The comparison reconstructs matching constraints from the actual graph,
rather than accepting the alternating-product surrogate unchecked.
