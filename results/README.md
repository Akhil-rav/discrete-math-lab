## Status labels

- **Reproduced** — matches a known result
- **Observed** — pattern found computationally
- **Conjectured** — proposed statement with no proof yet
- **Disproved** — false statement with a recorded counterexample
- **Proved** — rigorous proof completed

No claim is labeled new or open without a literature check.
