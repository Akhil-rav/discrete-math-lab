# Ladder study: source scope

Checked November 15, 2026. The question here fixes the host
$L_k=P_k\square K_2$, targets a spanning perfect matching, allows arbitrary
edge colorings, and maximizes the number of colors actually used. The finite
classification keeps edges fixed before taking a separate graph-symmetry
quotient.

Searches combined “ladder graph,” “rainbow perfect matching,” “anti-Ramsey,”
“matchings,” and “P_n K_2,” followed by exact-title searches for nearby
regular-bipartite results. These searches did not authenticate an external
theorem giving the three values or the complete orbit lists in this study.
This is an access and search limit, not evidence of novelty.

One adjacent primary publisher record is Zemin Jin,
[*Anti-Ramsey numbers for matchings in 3-regular bipartite graphs*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0096300316304751).
The publisher's indexed abstract concerns cubic bipartite hosts. Direct
full-page access returned HTTP 403; the theorem and its exceptions were not
checked in full text. Every ladder here has degree-two corners, so these
hosts are not 3-regular. No theorem from that paper is used as a premise.
The [cube source note](cube_classification.md) records a different, later
cubic-bipartite perfect-matching paper, also with a full-text access limit.

The [Young and matching notes](young_and_matchings.md) give the existing
complete-graph and complete-bipartite context. A theorem on those hosts does
not supply a fixed-ladder extremal classification. Likewise, a maximum over
a planar graph family would not automatically give the value on each ladder.

A further pass on November 15 searched for the weighted-cover formulation,
“color deficit,” and the general ladder formula together with “anti-Ramsey”
and “rainbow perfect matching.” No exact primary statement was authenticated.
The [new note](../matching_covers.md) supplies its own elementary proofs of
both the cover reduction and $A(L_k)=3k-5$ for $k\ge3$. The earlier cubic-host
access limits remain; absence from these searches does not establish novelty.

The later [direct five-column count](../l5_extremizers.md) uses only the
explicit eight matching sets and elementary case analysis. It does not import
an external classification theorem or change this search's novelty limits.

**Comparison status:** adjacent literature only; exact prior overlap is
unverified. The bounds are supported by the self-contained elementary proof,
and the classifications by the explicitly bounded computations. Systematic
MathSciNet, zbMATH, and forward-citation searches were not completed. No
novelty or currently-open-problem claim is made.

On November 16, a further search for fixed ladder hosts, edge-color
partitions, perfect matchings and extremal colorings located the publisher's
abstract of [*Anti-Ramsey number of matchings in outerplanar graphs*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2023.11.049),
*Discrete Applied Mathematics* 345 (2024), 125–135. Its stated parameter
maximizes over a family of Hamiltonian maximal bipartite outerplanar graphs.
Such a family maximum alone does not classify partitions of a specified host.
The full text was unavailable in this search, so its subsidiary results have
not been ruled out as prior work on ladders. The [ladder classification](../../papers/ladder_stability.pdf)
uses the earlier ladder bound, square contraction, and explicit finite bases.
