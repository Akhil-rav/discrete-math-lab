# Equal finite-horizon observations, different terminal support

**Proposition (working proof; priority unverified).** For every integer t >= 1, take any even k >= 16t+4. There are two reachable connected cubic states of the random greedy triangle-free process on 2k vertices, at the same step, with exactly the same joint distribution of the open-pair counts Q_0,...,Q_t when restarted at those states. From one state a terminal graph with k^2 edges has positive probability; from the other it has probability zero. Here time zero means the specified state, not the empty graph. The observation is only the sequence of counts, not the identities of chosen edges.

**Construction.** Form a strip with k columns of two vertices, a rung per column, and two rails per gap. Close the strip either straight or with the two rails interchanged at the seam. These simple cubic graphs are triangle-free: their small neighborhoods are those of the infinite two-row ladder. Every ordering of their 3k edges is legal from the empty graph and has positive probability. The straight closure admits the balanced checkerboard coloring since k is even. Adding all missing cross-edges in any fixed order reaches the maximal K(k,k) with positive probability. The twisted closure has an odd cycle: traverse k rails starting at one seam vertex, then return by its rung. Every extension retains this cycle. By the equality case of Mantel's theorem it cannot have k^2 edges. This part distinguishes support, not typical outcomes or a uniform lower bound on the probability.

**1. Small induced patterns have identical counts.** For R=8t the two graphs have identical induced rooted radius-R balls at every vertex: lift to the infinite strip. Column indices in a ball lie in an interval of span at most 2R, and k>2R+1 prevents both wraparound identification and extra edges between the interval ends. Thus the two graphs also have the same initial Q: the closed-or-present pairs from a vertex are exactly the other vertices at distance at most two.

For any connected test graph F on at most 8t vertices, fix one vertex of F. Its image may be chosen in 2k ways, and every homomorphism with that root image lies in its radius-R ball. So the homomorphism counts agree; for disconnected F multiply over components. Injective counts now agree by subtracting maps that identify vertices: partition maps by their exact kernel and induct on the number of vertices of the quotient (a loop gives zero). Finally inclusion-exclusion over undesired edges gives equality of counts of induced labeled embeddings of each such F. Specifying roles for some vertices of F does not affect this argument: embeddings already map individual named vertices.

**2. Count candidate histories by their marked neighborhoods.** An ordered list of t proposed pairs has at most 2t distinct endpoints. For counting, orient each proposed pair; repeated endpoints are allowed. Record the induced initial graph on all these endpoints and all their initial neighbors, marking each of the 2t endpoint positions in order. There are at most 8t vertices by cubicity. For each realizable marked type, every induced embedding of its underlying pattern produces an endpoint tuple with exactly the prescribed full neighborhoods: every marked vertex already has degree three inside the pattern and every unmarked vertex is a neighbor of a marked vertex. Conversely each such tuple comes from exactly as many embeddings as there are automorphisms of the pattern fixing the ordered marks. Hence the number of tuples of each type agrees by step 1.

**3. Recover path probabilities from the marked type.** Add proposed edges in order inside the pattern. At each stage all current neighbors of every marked vertex are known: the only added edges have marked endpoints. Thus legality of a proposed pair uv is known by adjacency and the common-neighbor test. On adding a legal uv, only uv itself and pairs from u to neighbors of v or from v to neighbors of u can cease to be open. All their vertices belong to the pattern. Each such candidate has a marked endpoint; to decide whether it was open, inspect the full neighbor list of that endpoint and test adjacency to the other endpoint inside the induced pattern. No knowledge of an unmarked vertex's outside neighbors is needed. Count distinct losses to update Q. The initial Q is shared, hence identical marked types yield identical Q histories and identical probabilities (product of reciprocal pre-move counts for the unoriented choices). Sum by type and divide by 2^t to undo the auxiliary orientations. Early termination cannot occur here: after t additions there remain at least 2k-2t untouched vertices of degree three, each with at most 3(3+t) vertices at distances one or two in total, fewer than 2k-1. This proves the proposition.

**Finite challenge to a stronger guess.** The script horizon.py enumerates every legal next edge and recomputes the whole open set from scratch. It compares joint path laws as exact fractions, not means. At k=4,6 the two closures already disagree after one step; k=8 agrees at one but disagrees at two; k=10,12 agree through two. These runs illustrate why equality at one time does not justify induction. The constant 16 is sufficient; sharpness is not claimed.

**Literature scope.** [Bohman, The triangle-free process (2009)](https://www.math.cmu.edu/~tbohman/papers/r3t.pdf) is the process reference. Homomorphism-to-induced-count conversion is standard; see [Lovasz, Large Networks and Graph Limits](https://www-labs.iro.umontreal.ca/~hahn/IFT3545/lovasz.pdf). Local indistinguishability is a classical tool; compare [Libkin, Elements of Finite Model Theory, chapter 4](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/libkin/fmt/fmt.pdf). The terminal obstruction uses only classical extremal graph theory. A bounded web search for the triangle-free process with finite-horizon open-pair histories, Mobius ladders, local statistics, lumpability and bipartite completions did not identify this exact combined statement. This is not an exhaustive search of stochastic graph rewriting, graph algebras, or finite model theory. A general finite-observation theorem could imply it. Neither priority nor publication-level significance has been established.
